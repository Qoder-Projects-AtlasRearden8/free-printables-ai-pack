Low effort, on purpose

Useful beats
impressive.

No course to finish. No streak to protect. No perfect prompt to memorize. Use one page the way you would a good sticky note.

01

Choose one

Find the situation that feels most like your week. Start there, not at the beginning of the pack.

02

Print less

Print one guide or a single page. Open the HTML on screen, use it as a fillable reference, or print the whole pack only when you actually want it.

03

Shrink the step

Change the brackets to fit your real life, then make the first action smaller than feels impressive. A five-minute version that happens beats a perfect plan that waits.

*

The rule for the whole pack: keep your judgment in the room. AI is a first draft, not an authority; a planner is a guess, not a promise. Check facts, protect private information, and let the next small step be enough.

Before you print

Small print,
plain English.

Is this really free?

Yes. No sign-up, paywall, email capture, or checkout. The files are static and this page has no analytics.

Can I share these with my class or clients?

Yes — print and share them for personal, classroom, or client use with the Asdale Dynamics credit intact. Please don’t resell or repackage the files as your own product.

Are these actually ADHD-friendly?

They are designed with low effort in mind: no streaks, no required order, short entry points, generous writing space, and explicit “stop” points. They are not a clinical treatment, assessment, or substitute for care from a qualified professional.

Will these work with my tools?

The AI principles work across assistants, though labels and features change. Check your own app’s current menu where a guide mentions a setting. The life-admin pages work on paper, in a notes app, or printed one page at a time.

Is this medical, legal, or financial advice?

No. These guides are educational. For medical care, talk to a qualified clinician. For legal, tax, benefits, or financial decisions, use an appropriate professional or official source. In an emergency, contact your local emergency service.

How should I print them?

The PDFs are US Letter, designed for 100% scale. On A4 paper, choose “fit to page.” Turn on background graphics for the intended look; the text and writing space still work without them. The gallery has one combined PDF and every guide separately.
